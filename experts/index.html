A few things to know

Trust starts
with clarity.

Can I book an expert today?

SaveRx Experts is in early access. We’re building the network and defining services. You can request support using the contact form. Submitting a request does not book a session or guarantee an expert match.

Who can join as an expert?

We welcome interest from pharmacists, radiologists, nurses, pharmacy technicians, patient access specialists, and healthcare operations professionals. Participation will depend on credential verification, relevant experience, and the scope of each engagement.

Can you help with prior authorization roadblocks?

Connect with an expert who understands prior authorization and patient access. They can help you make sense of a delay or denial, identify questions about missing information, and prepare next steps for following up with your prescriber, pharmacy, or insurer. Support is educational and navigational; approval decisions remain with your insurer and cannot be guaranteed.

Is this medical care?

The initial individual offering is education and healthcare navigation. It does not include diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, or interpretation of your scans. Any future clinical service would require appropriate licensing and operational safeguards.

How can experts earn through SaveRx?

Our planned marketplace lets qualified professionals offer paid 1:1 education and navigation sessions. Experts will review the scope, session format, and compensation before accepting. Rates, fees, and payment terms will be clear before work begins; earnings are not guaranteed.

How will pricing and privacy work?

Session pricing and expert compensation will be disclosed before an engagement. Personal information should only be shared with clear consent and within the agreed scope of support. Please do not include medical records or personal health information in an early-access inquiry.
