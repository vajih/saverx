Good questions.
Straight answers.

Is SaveRx a pharmacy or insurance?

No. SaveRx helps you discover manufacturer prescription savings programs. The manufacturer runs the program, determines eligibility, and handles enrollment. Your pharmacy fills your prescription.

Can I use these programs with my insurance?

It depends on the program. Many manufacturer copay cards require commercial insurance and exclude Medicare, Medicaid, and other government insurance. Patient assistance programs have different requirements, sometimes including income limits. Always confirm the terms on the official program website.

Are the savings guaranteed?

No. Your cost depends on eligibility, insurance coverage, medication, and program limits. Offers can change. Check the official program’s current terms before enrolling.

Do I need to share personal information to search?

You can explore SaveRx without an account or entering insurance information. Enrollment on a manufacturer’s website may require personal information under that program’s privacy policy.
